How it works

Your paste is compressed in your browser and stored in the URL fragment. GitHub Pages serves the app; no paste database or backend is used. Ordinary text compresses well, so longer pastes may fit in a share link.

Drafts stay in this browser

Unshared drafts are saved locally on this device to help prevent losing your work. They are not synced between devices.

Privacy warning

Anyone with the complete share link can read the paste. Compression is not encryption—never share passwords, API keys, private keys, or other sensitive information.

Link size and storage

This creates a compressed link, not a short-ID link. URL length depends on the content and browser limits; some pastes may still be too large. Short IDs, expiry, and server-side storage require a backend.
